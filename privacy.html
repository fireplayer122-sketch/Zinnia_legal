Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Zinnia uses, why it is used, and how it is handled when you interact with the Zinnia Discord bot or website.

1. Information Zinnia Uses

When you use the Discord bot, Zinnia may store information needed to provide its features, such as your Discord user ID, registration and account progress, cards and posters you own, card IDs, rarity, print numbers, levels, XP, ascensions, customization, currencies, inventory items, wishlists, selected cards and series, quests, achievements, Daily reward information, market activity, trades, gifts, reminders, and feature preferences.

This information is primarily associated with your Discord user ID rather than a separate Zinnia username or password.

2. Website Login

Zinnia's website may let you sign in through Discord. The website requests Discord's basic identify permission so it can receive the account information needed for login.

The website may use your Discord user ID, username or global display name, and avatar to recognize you and show information connected to your Zinnia account, such as your card collection. Zinnia does not receive your Discord password.

3. Login Sessions and Cookies

The website uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. During the current beta, login sessions may be remembered for up to 30 days. Logging out clears the website session.

4. Why Information Is Used

Zinnia uses stored information to:

  • Operate card collecting and progression systems.
  • Save your collection and account progress.
  • Process trades, market activity, rewards, quests, and achievements.
  • Display your collection on the website.
  • Provide reminders and user preferences.
  • Prevent abuse and investigate exploits.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve Zinnia.

5. Information Sharing

Zinnia does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed or shared only when reasonably necessary for Discord features and authentication, hosting, operating Zinnia, security and abuse prevention, or complying with valid legal requirements.

Public actions performed in Discord may naturally be visible to other users in the server where those actions occur.

6. Public Information

Some Zinnia information may be visible to other users as part of normal gameplay, including Discord usernames or display names, card collections, profiles, wishlists, market listings, trades, gifts, achievements, and card information intentionally displayed by Zinnia. Do not use Zinnia to submit sensitive personal information.

7. Data Retention

Game information may remain stored while it is needed to operate Zinnia and preserve account progress. Some records may also remain for security, transaction history, moderation, abuse prevention, or technical purposes. Retention practices may change as the beta and hosting setup develop.

8. Data Requests

You may contact Zinnia staff through the official support system if you have questions about information associated with your account or want to request deletion of data that Zinnia controls. Some information may need to be retained when reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, transaction integrity, or legal obligations.

Deleting Zinnia data does not delete your Discord account or information independently controlled by Discord.

9. Security

Zinnia uses reasonable technical measures for account sessions and service operation, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Never send your Discord password, token, authentication code, or other account credentials to Zinnia staff.

10. Third-Party Services

Zinnia relies on services outside its control, particularly Discord and website or hosting infrastructure. Those services have their own privacy practices and policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Zinnia adds or changes features. Significant changes should be announced through official Zinnia channels when practical.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be submitted through Zinnia's official support server or ticket system.
