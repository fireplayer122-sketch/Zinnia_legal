How to play

Zinnia is designed to be easy to start. Register, drop cards, grab one, and build from there.

01

Register

Use Zregister and choose whether you want the beginner tutorial.

02

Drop cards

Use Zd to drop cards and grab the one you want from the buttons.

03

Build your collection

Use Zc to browse cards and Zv CARD_ID to view one in detail.

04

Level & ascend

Earn XP, level your favorite cards, and ascend them through A1–A6.

05

Explore everything else

Quests, achievements, the market, gifting, profiles, the shop, tags, rarities, wishlists, and customization open up as you play.
